# Shop Owner Onboarding & Operation Guide
> **Vintha Print — Complete 10-Step Guide for Print Shop Owners**

Follow this step-by-step guide to take your print shop live with automatic, contactless printing.

---

### Step 1: Register Your Shop
1. Visit the platform homepage and click **Register Your Shop**.
2. Complete the onboarding wizard:
   - Provide your business name (e.g. *Om Sai Xerox & Cyber Cafe*).
   - Enter your mobile and WhatsApp contact numbers.
   - Enter your full shop address, city, state, and PIN code.
   - Choose your customized public URL slug: `/shop/om-sai-xerox`.

---

### Step 2: Set Your Printing Prices
Configure your exact shop prices under **Pricing Settings**:
- **A4 Black & White (Single Side):** e.g., ₹2.00
- **A4 Black & White (Double Sided):** e.g., ₹3.00
- **A4 Colour (Single Side):** e.g., ₹10.00
- **A4 Colour (Double Sided):** e.g., ₹15.00
- **A3 & Photo Rates:** Configure according to your machinery.
- **Convenience/Service Fee:** Optional per-order platform fee (e.g., ₹2.00).
- **Minimum Order Floor:** Minimum payable order amount (e.g., ₹5.00).

---

### Step 3: Configure PhonePe Payment Gateway
1. Navigate to **Payment Settings** in your owner dashboard.
2. Select your payment mode:
   - **Platform Collection Mode:** Instant setup; Vintha collects customer payments and settles them daily to your registered UPI/bank account.
   - **Direct Merchant Mode:** Enter your PhonePe Merchant ID, Client ID, and Salt Key for direct settlement.
3. Click **Test Payment Connection** to verify gateway readiness.

---

### Step 4: Install the Vintha Print Agent
1. On your Windows counter computer connected to your printers, download the **Vintha Print Agent** setup file.
2. Run the installer (`VinthaPrintAgent-Setup.exe`).
3. The agent will launch and sit in your Windows system tray.

---

### Step 5: Pair Your Computer
1. In your web dashboard, navigate to **Printer Setup** -> **Add Print Computer**.
2. Note the generated **6-digit pairing code** (e.g., `849201`).
3. Enter this code into the Vintha Print Agent window on your shop PC and click **Pair Computer**.
4. The dashboard will show your computer as **Online & Connected**.

---

### Step 6: Select Your Active Printer
1. In the agent interface, review the discovered hardware printers.
2. Select your primary Xerox machine (e.g. *Canon imageRUNNER 2525* or *HP LaserJet Pro*).
3. If setting up for the first time, keep **Virtual Spooler Test Mode** enabled to test without wasting paper.

---

### Step 7: Run a Test Print
1. Click **Send Test Print** in the Print Agent window.
2. Verify that the test page spools cleanly to your printer (or is written to your `vintha-print-output` folder in virtual mode).

---

### Step 8: Download Your Branded QR Posters
1. Navigate to **QR & Downloads** in your shop dashboard.
2. Select your desired formats:
   - **A4 Counter Poster** (Portrait / Landscape)
   - **Counter Standee Card**
   - **Window Sticker**
   - **WhatsApp & Instagram Stories**
3. Click **Download High-Res PDF** or **Download PNG**.

---

### Step 9: Display the QR Poster at Your Shop
1. Print the downloaded A4 poster in full color and laminate it.
2. Place the standee prominently on your counter, customer waiting area, or storefront glass.

---

### Step 10: Receive Your First Customer Order!
1. A customer scans your counter QR using any phone camera or Google Lens.
2. The customer uploads their PDF or image and chooses their print settings.
3. The customer pays instantly via PhonePe or UPI.
4. Your paired Windows computer automatically receives the job and starts printing silently!
5. Hand the warm sheets directly to the customer without touching their phone or USB drive!
